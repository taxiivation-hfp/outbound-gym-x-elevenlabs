Brunswick Iron & Barbell
MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT — TERMS AND CONDITIONS — Form MA-4 (rev. Mar 2026)
1. The Parties
This agreement is made between Brunswick Iron & Barbell Pty Ltd (ACN 641 882 019) of 88 Sydney Road,
Brunswick VIC 3056 (“the Club”) and the person named in the schedule overleaf (“the Member”).
The Club operates a second site at 12 Nicholson Street, Coburg. Members holding a Full membership may train
at either site. Access to the Coburg site is not included in the Off-Peak membership described in clause 4.
2. Access and Staffed Hours
The premises are accessible to members from 5:00am until 11:00pm on weekdays and from 7:00am until
8:00pm on weekends and public holidays. Reception is staffed between 9:00am and 6:00pm on weekdays only.
Outside staffed hours, entry is by member fob. The Club reserves the right to vary access hours with fourteen
(14) days' written notice.
3. Classes
Group classes are included in all membership types. Members must reserve a place through the Club's booking
app. Places are released 72 hours in advance. A member who fails to attend a reserved class on three
occasions in any calendar month may have booking privileges suspended for 14 days.
4. Fees and Membership Types
Membership Term Fee Access
Full 12 months $74.00 / month Both sites, all hours
Full 6 months $82.00 / month Both sites, all hours
Off-Peak membership 12 months $45.00 / month Brunswick only, 10am–3pm weekdays
Casual visit — $22.00 per visit Brunswick only
A joining fee of $59.00 applies to all new memberships and is not refundable. Fees are debited monthly in
advance by direct debit.
5. Term and Expiry
5.1 A fixed-term membership runs for the term stated in the schedule and expires at the end of that term. It
does not continue automatically.
5.2 The Club is not obliged to notify the Member that the term is approaching its end. It remains the
Member's responsibility to arrange a further term if continued access is desired.
5.3 A Member on a Rolling membership authorises the Club to debit the monthly fee until written
cancellation is received, subject to the notice period in clause 6.
6. Cancellation
Rolling memberships may be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Fixed-term memberships may not be
cancelled before the end of the term except on medical grounds supported by a certificate from a registered
medical practitioner, or on relocation more than 25km from either site supported by evidence of the new
address.
7. Suspension
A Member may suspend a fixed-term membership for a minimum of two weeks and a maximum of eight weeks
in any twelve month period. The term is extended by the period of suspension. A suspension fee of $5.00 per
week applies.
8. Liability and Conduct
The Member acknowledges that use of the premises and equipment carries inherent risk. The Member warrants
that they are medically fit to undertake physical exercise and will advise the Club of any condition that may affect
their safety. The Club excludes liability to the extent permitted by the Australian Consumer Law.
Correct footwear must be worn at all times. Weights must be returned to the racks. The Club may terminate this
agreement without refund for conduct that endangers or intimidates other members or staff.
9. Privacy
The Club collects personal information to administer this agreement. The Club's Privacy Policy is available at
reception and sets out how that information is held, used and disclosed, and how the Member may access or
correct it.
