Opening hours
We're open 5am to 10pm weekdays and 7am to 7pm weekends. Staffed from 6am to 8pm on weekdays.
If someone asks when it's quiet, the floor is quietest weekdays before 8am and after 7pm. Avoid recommending
the 5pm to 6:30pm window to anyone who says they hate a crowd.
Sites
Southside Strength Co. operates from Bentleigh, with sister gyms in Cheltenham and Mordialloc. A membership
covers all three.

Classes
All classes are included. Members book through the app, or we can book them in at the desk over the phone if
they'd rather.
Online training
We do not currently offer online or remote programming. If a member is moving away, direct them to the
Cheltenham or Mordialloc sites if either is closer, otherwise wish them well.
Renewals
Members on a fixed term who renew before their expiry date get 15% off their first month of the new term. This
is the only discount we give on an existing membership. Do not improvise anything else, and do not offer to
check with a manager.
Returning members
Anyone whose membership has lapsed is eligible for one free personal training session on rejoining. It needs to
be booked in with a trainer, so take their details and Amy will call them back to arrange a time.
Members who've gone quiet
For current members we haven't seen in a while, you can give them a guest pass to bring a mate in. Text them
the link. Don't read the code out over the phone, people always write it down wrong.
